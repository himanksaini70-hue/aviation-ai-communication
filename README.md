✈️ Aviation AI Communication
AI-Assisted Passenger Communication Workflow for Flight Disruptions

📌 Project Overview

An AI-assisted workflow for drafting, verifying, and updating passenger communications during flight disruptions. The project combines prompt engineering, source verification, human review, and multilingual consistency checks to support clear, accurate, and consistent airline communication.

🎯 Business Problem

Airline service teams must communicate changing flight information clearly and consistently across multiple channels. Incorrect timings, unsupported benefit claims, and inconsistent updates can create passenger confusion and reduce trust.

This project explores a structured approach to generating, evaluating, and updating passenger communications using fictional flight scenarios and policy rules.

🚀 Key Features
📧 Multi-Channel Communication: Passenger emails, public announcements, SMS messages, and service desk responses.
🧠 Prompt Engineering: Comparison of six prompting strategies to evaluate communication quality.
🔎 Factual Verification: Source-based checks for incorrect timings, unsupported claims, and missing information.
🔄 Dynamic Updates: Revision of passenger communications when expected departure times change.
🌐 English–Hindi Consistency: Verification of meaning, facts, timings, and tone across both languages.
♻️ Reusable Prompt Template: Testing a standardized email prompt on a separate fictional flight scenario.
⚙️ Project Workflow
🗂️ Establish the Facts: Identify scheduled timings, expected departure times, applicable fictional policies, and information gaps.
✍️ Generate Drafts: Use structured prompts to create passenger communications for different channels.
📊 Evaluate Outputs: Assess factual accuracy, completeness, clarity, tone, and consistency.
🛡️ Verify and Correct: Identify unsupported claims, flag unknown information, and revise inaccurate statements.
🔄 Update Communications: Apply revised operational timings consistently across all relevant messages.
🌐 Review Translations: Compare English and Hindi versions for factual and semantic consistency.
🧪 Test Reusability: Apply the reusable email prompt to a separate flight scenario and verify the results.
🧪 Verification Scenarios
Scenario	Required Verification
✈️ SB204: Scheduled departure at 2:00 PM; expected departure at 4:30 PM	Calculate the 2-hour-30-minute delay and assess eligibility against the fictional two-hour voucher policy.
🔄 SB204: Expected departure revised to 5:15 PM	Replace the earlier estimate and ensure the passenger update is revised to 4:15 PM, assuming the update is scheduled one hour before the revised estimate.
❓ Unconfirmed Details: Gate, refund, or hotel information unavailable	Flag missing information and avoid inventing details or making unsupported promises.
🛡️ Departure Guarantees	Distinguish estimated departure times from confirmed timings; never present an estimate as a guarantee.
✈️ SB310: Scheduled departure at 10:00 AM; expected departure at 11:15 AM	Calculate the 1-hour-15-minute delay and confirm that it falls below the supplied two-hour voucher threshold.

🤖 Responsible AI Use

AI is used to assist with drafting, evaluating, and translating passenger communications. The contributor independently verifies source facts, checks applicable policy rules, records corrections, and reviews final outputs before considering them ready for use.

🔒 Scope & Limitations: This project uses entirely fictional flight information and policies. It is not connected to live airline systems, does not access real-time flight data, and does not send messages to actual passengers.

🌟 Project Outcome

A structured, reusable AI-assisted communication workflow designed to improve the accuracy, clarity, consistency, and reliability of passenger updates during flight disruptions while keeping factual verification and final decision-making under human control.
